# The Lighthouse Keeper

A short sample book used to smoke-test the complete Itranslation pipeline.

## The First Storm

The lighthouse stood on a narrow rock at the edge of the sea. For forty years, its keeper had climbed the spiral stairs every evening to light the lamp.

No ship had been lost near the point since he arrived. The fishermen knew the white flash meant safety, and they trusted it the way they trusted the tide.

## The Missing Oil

One autumn morning, the keeper opened the storehouse and found the oil barrels empty. A storm was already gathering beyond the horizon, and the supply boat would not come for another week.

He walked along the cliff path to the village. The villagers listened quietly, then offered what little oil they could spare from their own lamps.

## The Long Night

That night the wind struck the coast with a roar. The keeper poured every drop of the precious oil into the lamp, and the beam cut through the rain like a silver spear.

When dawn finally came, the sea was calm again. Every boat in the harbor was safe, and the keeper knew that the light had done its work.
